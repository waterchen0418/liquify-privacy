修好的照片存到哪裡？

存回你的相簿，是一張新的照片，原本那張不會被覆蓋。

為什麼存檔按鈕按不了？

存檔只在你實際修改過照片之後才會啟用。如果還沒動過任何一筆，那個按鈕是灰的。

今天不能再存檔了？

免費版每天可以存兩張，每天重置。解鎖「無限次匯出」之後就沒有上限。 那是一次性買斷，不是訂閱，不會自動續訂。

換手機之後購買不見了

開啟 App，修改任一張照片後按存檔叫出購買頁，點最下方的「恢復購買」。 只要是同一個 Apple 帳號，就會恢復。

照片會被上傳嗎？

不會。所有運算都在你的裝置上完成，這個 App 沒有要求網路權限， 也沒有要求讀取你的相簿 —— 它只拿得到你主動選的那一張。 詳見隱私權政策。

不小心修過頭了

用「還原」筆刷塗回原樣，或按左下角的復原箭頭一步步退回。 垃圾桶圖示會清除所有修改，按下去之前會先問你一次。

支援哪些裝置？

iOS 17 以上的 iPhone。

我要退款

退款由 Apple 處理，不經過我。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 提出申請。

Built by one person. You'll usually hear back within two or three working days.

Where does the edited photo go?

Into your photo library, as a new photo. The original is never overwritten.

Why is the Save button greyed out?

Save only becomes available once you've actually changed something. Before your first stroke, it stays disabled.

I can't save any more today

The free version saves two photos a day, and the count resets daily. Unlocking unlimited exports removes the limit. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it does not auto-renew.

I got a new phone and my purchase is gone

Open the app, edit any photo and tap Save to bring up the purchase screen, then tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple Account, it will come back.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your device. The app asks for no network permission and does not ask to read your photo library — it only ever receives the single photo you pick. See the privacy policy.

I went too far with an edit

Use the Restore brush to paint the original back, or tap the undo arrow at the bottom left to step backwards. The trash icon clears everything, and asks you to confirm first.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

I'd like a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
